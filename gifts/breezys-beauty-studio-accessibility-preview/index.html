Answers at a glance

A few questions, answered.

Clear answers are another little way to make every customer feel cared for.

Does quantity 1 mean one nail or one whole set?

As confirmed by a customer of the studio: quantity 1 is one complete 10-nail set; quantity 2 represents two complete sets (20 nails total). The studio can confirm any special-order exceptions.

Do I need two different sizing pictures?

Yes. The studio requests one full-hand picture and one separate thumb close-up, both photographed from above with a US quarter placed alongside for scale.

Can I include my own nail-art inspiration?

Yes. The provided order form says you may upload up to three inspiration images showing your desired design, colors, or details.

Does this preview change how checkout works?

No. It is an optional design and accessibility concept, not an ordering system. Breezy's actual store and checkout remain unchanged.
