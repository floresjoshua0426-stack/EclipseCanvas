A clearer way to play.

01 — UNDERSTAND

Your power, at a glance.

Readable health, energy, earned progression and active technique. The in-game skill panel reads real cooldowns, energy costs, ranks and input bindings.

02 — SEE THE SURFACE

Material detail, not more hype.

22 texture maps, nine PBR materials, textured terrain in all six themes, curved character panels and cloth detail. Original procedural art, not a finished photorealistic character library.

03 — KEEP MOVING

Fidelity stays the default.

DX11, TAA, 1080p output at roughly 720p internal resolution. Limited near shadows have an off switch. 30 FPS is a target, not a measured minimum.

How do I publish the actual public download?

Create a release in the EclipseCanvas repository and attach the complete Fidelity ZIP. Use allah-a3-fidelity for this update, and optionally eclipse-fidelity-base for the approved base. Upload the small index.html through your existing website workflow. The page discovers real uploaded release assets; an empty host, rate limit or network failure never becomes a fake download link.

The ZIP contains Website/PUBLISH_RELEASE.ps1 for the GitHub CLI workflow and Docs/WEBSITE_PUBLISHING.md for the browser workflow. No token, password or uploader credential belongs in this public HTML.

04 / What has—and has not—been checked

Evidence over promises.

Test receipts inside the archive distinguish native simulation, local packaging checks and test adapters from an actual Unreal build.

Native regressionProgression, reset, travel, combat and presentation geometry
Protected stagingSource hashes, asset receipts and isolated save copies
Hardware pendingUnreal rendering, controller hardware and Iris Xe frame pacing
Why not just turn on baked lighting everywhere?

The live sandbox generates and changes terrain, buildings and actors. It cannot receive a correct editor Lightmass bake for arbitrary future geometry. A3 retains dynamic live-world rendering. A separate fixed-scene CPU Lightmass workflow is documented in the ZIP; no baked lighting is falsely claimed for all realms.

Is this a finished photorealistic or universally compatible game?

No. It is a complete development source snapshot with better surface materials, a revised procedural hero and improved interface. Open industrial frames remain open geometry, not fully modeled skyscrapers. Finished photorealistic faces, motion-capture libraries, seamless full-scale planets, multiplayer and guaranteed frame rates are not included. The game must be built and playtested on Windows.
