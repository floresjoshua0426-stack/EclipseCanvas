01 / The sandbox

Systems that meet each other.

Built on the playable Flow1 foundation. The additions below are implemented in source and tested in the portable simulation; their final Unreal presentation still needs device testing.

01 — MOVE

Flight with a sense of scale.

Three surface-flight gears, a forward flight pose, orbital boost and automatic planet approach. The visual atlas connects stars, planets, local activity and realm gates.

Travel uses a readiness-gated flight corridor between local worlds—not a continuously flyable spherical planet.

02 — INTERVENE

Conflicts with consequences.

Retained resident jobs and social systems, plus bounded faction patrols, deliveries, races and skirmishes. Rival successors and changing outcomes give the next visit a reason.

The current region is simulated actively. Every distant universe is not running simultaneously.

03 — EXPERIMENT

Skills first. Strength earned.

Use all 24 techniques from the start. Grow their strength, build mastery, discover eight relics, or selectively override progression with local OP controls.

Procedural effects and character prototypes are included; finished cinematic character art is not.

02 / A long-term horizon

From local hero to all-realm threat.

No mandatory campaign. Level 7000 is the ultimate progression target, with Universal followed by System / all realms.

Every skill is available at level one

500 hours is the balancing target.

The cap is 3,600,000 earned XP. A reference rate of 7,200 XP per active hour reaches it in 500 hours. Favors, conflicts, exploration, puzzles and real destruction contribute; passive time does not.

This is a calibrated reward budget, not 500 hours of human playtesting. Real pacing depends on activity density and play style. OP overrides keep earned progression separate.

At level 7000, every power-family rank reaches 100. The Big Bang rebirth unlocks a confirmed simulation reset: keep your character progression or start fresh, with the previous world cycle retained.

Rebirth VFX are procedural. No cinematic movie or whole-planet physics-fracture system is bundled. Reset uses protected save epochs, not deletion of your installation.

First-time visitors: what is required?

This is a development source release for an already prepared ECLIPSE project, not a public game.exe release. You need the existing prepared map and imported assets, Unreal 5.8.3 with its C++ toolchain, and sufficient disk space for a separate project/asset copy. The source includes earlier preparation tools, but a fresh setup and a packaged Windows game are not verified by this release.

Official setup: Get Unreal Engine from Epic · Epic's Visual Studio setup guide · Hardware and rendering requirements

Where are the new controls and reset choices?

F6: visual atlas. F7: requests, progression and selective OP. F: flight. N: surface gear. Shift: boost. PageUp/PageDown: climb/descend. R: tether. New special actions are available as menu buttons and NumPad 1–3; NumPad 4 retains the original pulse. Original Flow1 menus, skills and controls remain.

At effective level 7000, arm the Big Bang, choose Keep level or Fresh progression, then confirm the current request. A separate confirmed action returns to the previous saved cycle. At most 64 protected cycles are retained per save root; none are automatically deleted. Test on the launcher's isolated progress copy first.

The ZIP includes ALLAH_A1_GUIDE.md with controller mappings, ability rules, save behavior and device acceptance checks. Physical Bluetooth, DualSense reconnect and haptics have not been tested here.

What is still development work?

Finished photorealistic heroes and motion-capture libraries; anatomical dismemberment; continuously traversable planet-sized spheres; no-hitch guarantees; full planet destruction; skyline train/flying-car city networks; multiplayer; and unlimited newly generated universes. The new event species and several powers use procedural prototypes. See the 23-entry ALLAH_A1_COVERAGE.json in the ZIP.

There is no browser game or server-side story generator on this page. This hub only explains the release and delivers files. Your sandbox no longer instantiates the mandatory narrative component.
